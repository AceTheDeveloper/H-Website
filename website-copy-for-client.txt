About Us page, story paragraph 1:
H Breakfast to Bar started as a breakfast spot with a short menu and a long
line on weekends. We kept hearing the same question from regulars: what
happens after lunch? So we found out. The kitchen stayed open, the bar went
in, and the same crew that flips your pancakes in the morning is often the
one closing out the bar at night.

About Us page, story paragraph 2:
Nothing about the food changed in spirit. It's still simple, made from
scratch, and meant to be eaten with people you like. We just stopped
closing at 2pm.
