ABOUT US PAGE
==============================================

Title area
----------

[Where: Top of the About Us page, big black headline]
Headline: A breakfast counter that never quite closed
Side text: How a short morning menu turned into a full day-to-night restaurant and bar.

Our story
---------

[Where: Cream section with a photo of guests at their tables on the right]
Heading: Same crew, from the first pour to last call

H Breakfast to Bar started as a breakfast spot with a short menu and a long line on weekends. We kept hearing the same question from regulars: what happens after lunch? So we found out. The kitchen stayed open, the bar went in, and the same crew that flips your pancakes in the morning is often the one closing out the bar at night.

Nothing about the food changed in spirit. It's still simple, made from scratch, and meant to be eaten with people you like. We just stopped closing at 2pm.

The wall
--------

[Where: Big red section in the middle of the About Us page]
Huge text: Live live local daily.
It's painted on the wall of our dining room, and it's the short version of everything on this page.

What we care about
------------------

[Where: Cream section, heading on the left and four lines on the right]
Heading: Simple food, made properly
Cooked from scratch: Sauces, dressings and bread are made in-house daily. Nothing comes out of a bag.
Sourced close by: Produce and coffee come from growers and roasters within a few hours of the kitchen.
One table, all day: You can sit down for breakfast and still be there for last call. Nobody rushes you.
Behind the bar: Our bartenders build the cocktail list the same way the kitchen builds the menu.
